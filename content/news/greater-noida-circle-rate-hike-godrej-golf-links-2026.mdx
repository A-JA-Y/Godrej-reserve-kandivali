  In a move that has sent ripples through Greater Noida's luxury property market, the Greater Noida Authority approved a 3.58% revision in circle rates during its 143rd board meeting held on May 3, 2026 — the first such revision in nearly nine years. For homebuyers, investors, and existing owners at Godrej Golf Links Greater Noida, this is a moment that demands close attention.

  The revision, aligned with the Cost Inflation Index, is being viewed as a long-overdue correction that closes the gap between government-notified rates and the booming market prices seen across Greater Noida's premium residential zones. Godrej Golf Links, with its iconic 9-hole golf course spread across a 100-acre township in Sector 27, sits squarely at the intersection of this policy shift.

  ## Why this revision matters right now
  <a id="importance"></a>

  Circle rates — the minimum benchmark valuations set by the government for property registration — had remained frozen since 2016 even as actual market prices surged dramatically. Over the past few years, a widening chasm had emerged between these static official rates and the real transaction values at premium projects like Godrej Golf Links. The revision signals the government's intent to begin catching up — and signals confidence in the market.

  ## Infrastructure push amplifies the circle rate signal
  <a id="infrastructure"></a>

  The circle rate revision came alongside an even more consequential announcement: the Greater Noida Authority cleared a proposal for a 37-km, 105-metre-wide road connecting Greater Noida to the Hapur bypass, with further linkage to the Ganga Expressway — the arterial highway joining Meerut to Prayagraj. The corridor will pass through key nodes including Sector Alpha 2, NH-91, and the Eastern Peripheral Expressway, and is estimated to be completed within two to three years.

  Combined with the upcoming Jewar International Airport — targeted for a July 2026 opening and just 10 km from Godrej Golf Links Greater Noida — and the proposed metro extension from Pari Chowk to Jewar (completion by 2028), the infrastructure context surrounding this project is arguably the most compelling it has ever been.
