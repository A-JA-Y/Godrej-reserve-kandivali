  Godrej Reserve, Godrej Properties' 18.6-acre residential development on Akurli Road in Kandivali East, Mumbai, had reached approximately 50% construction completion as of July 2026. The developer is targeting possession in June 2030, while the completion date registered with MahaRERA is December 2030.

  The towers rise approximately 51 floors over a ground-plus-eight-level podium. Commencement certificates are in place, and the occupancy certificate will be applied for closer to possession.

  ## From land deal to launch to half-built
  <a id="timeline"></a>

  Godrej Properties acquired the freehold land parcel outright in December 2022, with a developable potential of about 3.72 million sq.ft. and an estimated revenue potential of roughly ₹7,000 crore. The project launched in March 2024 and sold about ₹2,690 crore of inventory at launch — the developer's best-ever Mumbai launch by value and volume, announced in April 2024.

  ## What it means for buyers
  <a id="buyers"></a>

  Godrej Reserve is sold on a 25:25:50 construction-linked payment plan — 10% as the booking amount, 15% within 45 days of booking, 25% in March 2027, 40% on application of the Occupancy Certificate and 10% on intimation of possession. Buyers should plan around the December 2030 MahaRERA completion date and treat June 2030 as the developer's target.

  A bank-subvention scheme offering no EMI for two years from the Agreement for Sale was advertised with a stated window till 31 March 2026 — confirm whether any such offer is still available before relying on it.
