## Reading a Godrej Reserve Cost Sheet

<a id="cost-sheet"></a>

Two kinds of numbers circulate for Godrej Reserve, and they are not directly comparable:

- **The official starting price — ₹5.94 Cr.+\* for a 3 BHK.** Published by Godrej Properties (January 2026), it is an indicative agreement value; stamp duty, registration, GST and other charges are extra.
- **All-inclusive market quotes.** 2026 market cost sheets quote all-in figures by carpet area, floor and view, from about ₹4.85 Cr for a compact 3 BHK to ₹9.40 Cr for a large 4 BHK.

## What Moves the Price of a Unit

<a id="factors"></a>

- **Carpet area and configuration:** 3 BHKs run from 1,126 to 1,471 sq.ft. and 4 BHKs from 2,009 to 2,039 sq.ft.
- **Wing and floor:** prices vary by wing and floor, so ask for the floor-rise chart.
- **View:** decks facing the central greens or the national-park skyline can be priced differently.
- **Taxes and charges:** stamp duty, registration, GST and other charges sit on top of the agreement value.
- **Offers:** subvention and other schemes change over time — confirm what applies on the day you book.

## Paying for It

<a id="payment"></a>

The construction-linked plan runs 25:25:50 — 10% as the booking amount, 15% within 45 days of booking, 25% in March 2027, 40% on application of the Occupancy Certificate and 10% on intimation of possession. The project is approved with SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank and others for funding of up to 90%, subject to eligibility, and all-in cost sheets report a minimum down payment of around ₹45 lakh. ## Weighing It Up

<a id="verdict"></a>

The premium is attached to scale, open space, metro access and the developer's brand — features that are hard to replicate in Kandivali East. On the other side of the ledger, the project is under construction (about 50% complete as of July 2026), possession is targeted for June 2030 with a MahaRERA completion date of December 2030, and published offers come with time windows. Weigh the premium against your own timeline and budget; this is not investment advice.
