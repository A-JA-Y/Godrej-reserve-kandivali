## About Godrej Golf Links

<a id="about"></a>

Godrej Golf Links is a 100-acre integrated golf township located in Sector 27, Greater Noida, developed by <a href="https://godrejgolflink.in" className="text-blue-600 underline"> Godrej  Properties</a> — India's first ISO-certified real estate company with over 31 years of proven delivery. The project is one of the most distinctive residential communities in North India, built around a signature 9-hole golf course inspired by the iconic St. Andrews Golf Course in Scotland.

The township features a thoughtfully planned mix of independent villas (V2, V3, V4, V5 configurations) and low-rise apartment clusters, all arranged around an expansive central green spine. With Over 60% of the total area dedicated to open green spaces, the density is kept deliberately low — resulting in an environment that genuinely feels like a private resort rather than a crowded residential colony.

## Villa Types & Configurations

Godrej Golf Links is structured into several distinct villa clusters, each with its own character, size range, and price positioning. Here is a complete breakdown:

<a id="villas"></a>

### V3 — Crest Villas

The entry point into the villa lifestyle at Godrej Golf Links. Crest offers smartly designed 3 and 4 BHK configurations with elegant architecture, private gardens, and golf course proximity. Ideal for nuclear families or couples who want to downsize from larger configurations without compromising on quality.

### V4 — Evoke Villas

The most popular and widely chosen configuration in the township. Evoke Villas offer generously sized 4 BHK layouts spread across 4,177–5,335 sq. ft., featuring engineered marble flooring in living areas, wooden flooring in bedrooms and terraces, spacious balconies with glass railings, and private lawns. Available with fully furnished options including modular kitchens, air conditioning, wardrobes, and appliances.

### V5 — Exquisite & Presidential Villas

The crown jewels of the township. Exquisite and Presidential Villas are ultra-premium 4 BHK and 5 BHK homes with double-height living areas, sweeping golf course views, larger plot sizes, premium imported interiors, and optional personal elevators. These residences are designed for discerning buyers for whom only the absolute finest will do. Layouts range from 3,745 sq. ft. to 5,376 sq. ft. and beyond.

### Windsor — Low-Rise Apartments

British-colonial style 3 and 4 BHK residences with large balconies, tree-lined avenues, and classic colonial architecture. Windsor is the preferred choice for investors seeking rental income, as the lower price point combined with strong township amenities generates consistent tenant demand from corporate professionals and expats.


### The Suites

Premium studio and compact apartments within the township ecosystem — ideal for young professionals, investors seeking passive income, or parents who want to be close to their children living in the main villa clusters.

### The Jewar Airport effect
The Noida International Airport at Jewar is located approximately 10 km from Godrej Golf Links and is accessible in roughly 20 minutes via the Yamuna Expressway. Now nearing completion in 2026, it is expected to be one of Asia's largest airports, directly catalysing demand for premium residential real estate in a 20 km radius. Historically, properties within this radius of major new airports see 40–60% appreciation in the 3–5 years post-opening. Godrej Golf Links is squarely in that zone.

Beyond the airport, the region's infrastructure story is unusually rich: a proposed metro extension from Pari Chowk to Jewar (target 2028), a proposed Film City near Yamuna Expressway, and a major Logistics and Warehousing Park — all within the same corridor. Each of these projects independently increases employment density, which in turn drives housing demand and rental income.

Godrej Properties has received over 400 awards and is consistently India's most trusted developer brand. In resale markets, Godrej-branded properties command a measurable price premium over competing projects — typically 8–12% above market rate.
