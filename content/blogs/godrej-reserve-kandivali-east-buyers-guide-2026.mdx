Godrej Reserve is Godrej Properties' flagship residential development in Mumbai's western suburbs: an 18.6-acre freehold site on Akurli Road, Kandivali East, directly opposite Growel's 101 Mall. Towers of roughly 52 storeys rise over an eight-level podium, leaving nearly 6 acres of landscaped greens across two levels — the reason the project promises "an endless vacation at home."

This guide brings together the verified facts a buyer needs — configurations, carpet areas, pricing, payment terms, amenities, connectivity, possession and RERA registrations — so you can shortlist with clarity before you visit the sales lounge.

---

## About Godrej Reserve

<a id="about"></a>

In December 2022, Godrej Properties acquired the 18.6-acre land parcel outright — one of the largest luxury land purchases in Mumbai's western suburbs, with a developable potential of about 3.72 million sq.ft. and an estimated revenue potential of roughly ₹7,000 crore. The project launched in March 2024 and sold about ₹2,690 crore of homes at launch, Godrej Properties' best-ever Mumbai launch by value and volume.

Six towers are planned on the master plan and five wings are registered with MahaRERA so far. Each floor holds just eight residences served by seven lifts, and a street-front retail plaza with units of 450–2,500 sq.ft. completes the plan.

## Configurations & Carpet Areas

<a id="configurations"></a>

Every 3 and 4 BHK residence opens onto a private deck that extends the living room into the open air, and homes are Vastu-friendly.

### 3 BHK — 1,126 to 1,471 sq.ft. carpet

Units of 1,126, 1,149, 1,332, 1,450, 1,457 and 1,471 sq.ft. The mid-size 3 BHKs (1,126–1,332 sq.ft.) suit families upgrading within Kandivali; the 1,450+ sq.ft. "grande" 3 BHKs offer en-suite bedrooms and a wider deck.

### 4 BHK — 2,009 to 2,039 sq.ft. carpet

Units of 2,009, 2,033 and 2,039 sq.ft. — corner residences with a walk-in wardrobe (approx. 5' × 10'), a utility room and panoramic decks facing the central greens or the national-park skyline.

### 2 BHK — limited

The 750–822 sq.ft. 2 BHKs offered in the first phase are largely sold out, and the official headline now markets 3 and 4 BHK homes. Enquire for resale or new-wing releases.

## Payment Plan & Home Loans

<a id="payment"></a>

- **Construction-linked plan (25:25:50):** 10% as the booking amount, 15% within 45 days of booking, 25% in March 2027, 40% on application of the Occupancy Certificate and the final 10% on intimation of possession.
- **Bank-subvention scheme:** the developer advertised a plan to register the Agreement for Sale within 30 days and pay no EMI for 2 years from the AFS date, with a stated window till 31 March 2026. It is subject to bank discretion and withdrawal, so confirm the current status at enquiry.
- **Home loans:** the project is approved with SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank and others, with funding of up to 90% subject to eligibility.
- **Down payment:** all-in cost sheets report a minimum down payment of around ₹45 lakh.

Model your own loan amount, tenure and rate with the EMI calculator on the [price page](/price).

## Possession, Construction & RERA

<a id="rera"></a>

Construction was about 50% complete as of July 2026. Possession is targeted for June 2030, while the MahaRERA completion date is December 2030 — plan around the RERA date and treat the earlier date as the developer's target.

The five registered wings are Wing 1 — P51800054703, Wing 2 — P51800079457, Wing 3 — P51800055694, Wing 4 — P51800054691 and Wing 5 — P51800054690. Verify each on [maharera.maharashtra.gov.in](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in) before booking.

## Checklist Before You Book

<a id="checklist"></a>

- **Price basis:** confirm whether a quote is the agreement value or an all-inclusive figure with stamp duty, registration, GST and other charges.
- **Wing and RERA number:** note the MahaRERA number of the wing you are buying in and check its completion date.
- **Floor and view:** ask for the floor-rise chart and how the view — central greens or skyline — affects the price.
- **Offers:** check with your bank whether the subvention scheme is still available before relying on it.
- **Plans:** request the detailed unit plan and tower-wise placement for the exact unit you shortlist.
